𑇈 About the Śāradā Separator (शारदा-विभाजकः)

The character 𑇈 (Unicode U+111C8, Sharada Separator) is an ancient inter-word punctuation mark traditionally utilized in Kashmiri Śāradā manuscripts and epigraphy. Unlike modern European-style spaces which break the natural visual continuity of Devanagari shiro-rekhā (top line), the Śāradā separator marks word or phrase divisions cleanly while preserving traditional manuscript cadence.
